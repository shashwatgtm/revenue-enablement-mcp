The case in brief

Our product.

What this buyer will check

In retail and e-commerce, the losses a risk case can count are a failed peak season, lost or late orders, returns and chargebacks. Operations, finance and the technology lead check the cost per order, the peak season and the link to the store platform.

Value drivers for retail

The driver you chose is described below.

Cost reduction

Less money spent on the work or on the failure it causes today: hours, rework, errors, outside spend.

  • Counted as: what the problem or the current process costs the buyer in a year (current annual cost) and the share they expect to remove (expected improvement percent).
How the return will be worked out

No return is shown yet because the two numbers it rests on can only come from the buyer: what the problem costs them in a year, and the share of that cost our product would remove. This tool does not fill them with example hours, headcount, rates or industry averages. Once the buyer gives them, the yearly value is that cost times that share (or the yearly value the buyer states directly). The return is that value less the price you gave ($90,000 a year), compared with the price; payback is the number of months of value it takes to cover the price; and the three-year figure is three years of value less three years of price. Each is also shown at half and at one and a half times the value, so the buyer sees how much the case depends on the share they chose.

The numbers to ask for
  1. What does the problem cost the buyer in a year today, in people's time, rework, errors and outside spend? This is current annual cost.
  2. What share of that cost does the buyer expect our product to remove, and what is that based on (their own history, a pilot)? This is expected improvement percent.
  3. If the buyer can value the result directly, what is it worth to them in a year? This is annual value estimate, and it can replace the two numbers above.

Which software builds an ROI business case?

The method matters more than the software. Write down what the buyer pays, the one value driver you can measure, today's baseline and the figure the buyer's finance team will check, then show the return at a low, an expected and a high case.

Helix does not rank vendors or name software to buy. Run this free builder for a first draft, check that every assumption has a source, an owner on the buyer's side and a date before you send it, or book 45 minutes.

Who is ROI Business Case Builder not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above; the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want the tools to read their CRM, email or call notes: they work only from what you type.
  • Sending anything straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.

How does ROI Business Case Builder work?

No sign-in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run ROI Business Case Builder.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
