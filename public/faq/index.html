Revenue Enablement: questions and answers

Short answers you can check against the code and the privacy policy.

By Shashwat Ghosh, Co-Founder and Fractional CMO, Helix GTM Consulting · Last updated 28 September 2026

Who is Revenue Enablement not ideal for?

  • Teams that want the tools to read their CRM, email or call notes: they work only from what you type.
  • Sending anything straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.
  • Teams that need saved deal history: nothing is stored, so keep each result in your own deal record.

How do I use Revenue Enablement?

  • In your browser: open a tool above and press Fill in an example to see how it works, then run it with your own details.
  • In Claude or ChatGPT: add Revenue Enablement once as a custom connector, with no sign-in, then ask in plain words. Use it in Claude or ChatGPT has the steps.

Cost, privacy and how it works

What is Revenue Enablement?

Revenue Enablement is a free web app and AI connector with twelve sales tools, by Shashwat Ghosh of Helix GTM Consulting. Give it an account, a deal or a competitor, and it drafts account plans, deal strategy, discovery questions, ROI business cases, mutual action plans, win-loss reviews, proposal sections, emails, demo scripts, negotiation guides, champion kits and competitive traps.

Is Revenue Enablement free, and do I need an account?

It is free, and there is no account or sign-in. Use the tools in your browser here, or add them to Claude or ChatGPT as a connector.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. Nothing is stored and nothing you type is logged. The privacy page has the details.

How do I use Revenue Enablement in Claude?

In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, select + and then Add custom connector. Name it Revenue Enablement, paste https://revenue-enablement.gtmhelix.com/mcp and choose no sign-in. The page Use it in Claude or ChatGPT covers Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code and Windsurf too.

Why do some answers look like templates?

Several tools return a structured plan with [placeholders] where your inputs did not give a fact. When an answer shows a benchmark, target, rate, weight or other figure you did not supply, it is labelled "Example figure: replace with your own". Figures calculated from your inputs are not labelled. Suggested lengths, timings and counts are marked as suggestions. Replace every example and placeholder before you use the result.

Can I call the tools from my own code?

Yes. Send POST https://revenue-enablement.gtmhelix.com/api/tools/<tool name> with a JSON body {"input": {...}}. The Docs page lists every field. Please keep to the rate limit of 30 requests per minute.

Is the source code public?

Yes, at https://github.com/shashwatgtm/revenue-enablement-mcp under the MIT licence. The npm package @shashwatgtmalpha/revenue-enablement-mcp runs these tools locally; it can lag behind this site until a new version is published.
