Executive Summary

Proposal for Example Logistics Co

This proposal outlines how Lanehop can help Example Logistics Co with the challenges below.

The Opportunity

Key challenges for Example Logistics Co:

  • About 1 in 6 deliveries fail on the first attempt
  • Each city plans routes in its own spreadsheet
  • Dispatchers spend hours re-planning routes when orders change
Our Recommendation

What Lanehop offers Example Logistics Co:

  • Routes re-planned in under a minute
  • One dispatch view for every city
  • Live in six weeks without IT help

Investment Overview

[Pricing: add yours or point to your pricing section]

Why Lanehop

The recommendation above lists what sets Lanehop apart. Add one piece of evidence for each point before you send this; in logistics tech, the evidence that lands is this: a before and after of cost per delivery or first-attempt delivery at one hub, measured over a full cycle of busy and quiet weeks..

Next Steps

We recommend the following path forward:

  1. Review this proposal and provide feedback
  2. Schedule a working session to finalize scope
  3. Begin implementation planning

We look forward to partnering with Example Logistics Co to achieve these outcomes.

Sector notes: logistics tech

Who is Proposal Section Writer not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above; the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want the tools to read their CRM, email or call notes: they work only from what you type.
  • Sending anything straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.

How do I use Proposal Section Writer from Claude?

Add Revenue Enablement once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. A prompt built from the tested example:

Use Revenue Enablement's Proposal Section Writer tool. section type: Executive summary; your solution: Lanehop; customer name: Example Logistics Co; customer industry: Third-party logistics; tone: Consultative; customer challenges: about 1 in 6 deliveries fail on the first attempt, each city plans routes in its own spreadsheet and dispatchers spend hours re-planning routes when orders change; key differentiators: routes re-planned in under a minute, one dispatch view for every city and live in six weeks without IT help.

How does Proposal Section Writer work?

No sign-in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Proposal Section Writer.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
