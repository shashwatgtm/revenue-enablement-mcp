Email Sequence Generator

Write a sequence of several emails

For SDRs and demand generation teams. The decision: what do we send, and when, to open a conversation?

Give it the sequence type, the buyer you are writing to and your solution, and get a series of emails with the day to send each one, plus tips for the sequence.

Who is the Email Sequence Generator tool not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above, because the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want the tool to read their CRM, email or call notes: it works only from what you type.
  • Anyone who wants to send the output straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.

How do I use the Email Sequence Generator tool with an AI assistant?

Add Revenue Enablement once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. Here is a prompt built from the tested example:

My role is not stated. Ask me for my role first. Use Revenue Enablement's Email Sequence Generator tool. sequence type: Cold outreach; target persona: heads of last-mile operations; target industry: third-party logistics; your solution: our product; tone: Professional. If this tool is unavailable, ask me to open its web page; do not invent its output. In Gemini CLI or Antigravity, use the Helix GTM Suite skills package for related guidance, without claiming to run this connector.

How does the Email Sequence Generator tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Email Sequence Generator.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
