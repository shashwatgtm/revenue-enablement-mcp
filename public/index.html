What is Revenue Enablement? Revenue Enablement is a free web app and AI connector with twelve sales tools for CROs and demand generation heads. It is built by Shashwat Ghosh of Helix GTM Consulting, an AI native fractional CMO who builds his own GTM tools. Give it an account, a deal or a competitor, and it drafts account plans, discovery questions, ROI business cases, emails and demo scripts. To use it in a supported MCP client, add https://revenue-enablement.gtmhelix.com/mcp; you do not need to sign in.

Questions people ask

Is there a free MCP server for sales enablement, such as account plans, deal strategy and win and loss reviews?

Yes. Revenue Enablement, by Helix GTM Consulting, has twelve sales tools: account plans, deal strategy, discovery questions, ROI business cases, mutual action plans, win and loss reviews, proposal sections, email sequences, demo scripts, pricing negotiation guides, champion enablement kits and competitive discovery questions. It is for CROs and demand generation heads, and every tool returns a draft to review and edit.

Is there a free ROI business case builder for B2B deals that works in supported MCP clients?

The ROI Business Case Builder uses the buyer's annual value estimate, or their current annual cost and expected improvement percentage, together with the solution price. With those figures it calculates ROI, payback and sensitivity. Without them it asks for the missing figures and gives the case structure, without a calculated return.

What free AI tool writes discovery questions and a mutual action plan?

In Revenue Enablement, the Discovery Question Bank writes questions for each part of the framework you use, such as MEDDPICC, BANT or SPICED. The Mutual Action Plan Generator builds a close plan with stakeholders, success criteria, dated phases, risks and a communication plan.

How do I use Revenue Enablement in supported MCP clients, and is it free?

Revenue Enablement is free to use, with no account, sign in or API key. In ChatGPT, install the Helix Sales Enablement plugin from the ChatGPT plugin directory. Or add https://revenue-enablement.gtmhelix.com/mcp as a custom connector in Claude (web, desktop or mobile), in ChatGPT on plans that allow developer mode, or in Cursor, VS Code or Windsurf; in Claude Code, run claude mcp add --transport http revenue-enablement https://revenue-enablement.gtmhelix.com/mcp. You can also run the tools in a browser on this site.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. What you type and the answer are not stored, and nothing you type is logged. Only an anonymous count of tool calls is kept. The privacy page has the details.

How to read the answer

Each tool returns a structured sales asset built from fixed rules, such as an account plan, deal health score, discovery question bank, ROI business case, or dated close plan. Figures calculated from your inputs are shown directly, while missing facts appear as bracketed placeholders and any unprovided benchmark is labelled as an example figure to replace with your own.

What to do next

  • Founder: Use Discovery Question Bank, ROI Business Case Builder, and Demo Script Builder to structure founder-led sales calls and business cases.
  • CEO: Use ROI Business Case Builder, Mutual Action Plan Generator, and Win/Loss Analyzer to review strategic deals and why deals are won or lost.
  • CMO: Run Competitive Trap Setter, Champion Enablement Kit, and Email Sequence Generator to align sales collateral and outbound sequences with marketing positioning.
  • CRO: Roll out Account Plan Builder, Deal Strategy Coach, Pricing Negotiation Guide, and Mutual Action Plan Generator across deal reviews and forecast calls.
  • Demand generation head: Use Email Sequence Generator and Discovery Question Bank to script SDR outreach and qualification handoffs.

Why come back

Re-run these tools whenever a deal moves to a new stage, a buyer asks for an ROI case or discount, or you close a quarter and run win/loss reviews. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.
