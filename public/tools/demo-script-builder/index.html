Part 1: Opening (4 minutes, from 0:00)

Say: "Thanks for making the time. Over the next 30 minutes I will take you through our product, with the Head of Last-Mile Operations in the room, with Third-party logistics in mind."

Say: "What I would like by the end of this session is a clear next step that we both agree."

Say: "The plan: first I confirm that I have your problem right, then 16 minutes of demo in 3 steps (the workflow the buyer described, end to end, with their own example, the controls, reports and audit trail the buyer's reviewers will ask for and where it sits among the systems the buyer already runs), then your questions, then the next step."

After The Demo
  • Send the summary promised in the close, and brief your champion separately.

To sharpen this, give: attendees (it would add a row and a question for each person, and choose the parts they should see); key pain points (it would choose which parts are shown and what the playback says; now the steps follow the order of your description); competitor context (it would add a side by side line and sharpen the difference answers); must show features (it would fix the flows that are always shown; now the steps come from the usual demo list of this kind of seller); known objections (it would add a spoken answer to each; none is answered now); desired outcome (it would change the close; now it agrees the next step); your solution (list the parts of the product with a few words on what each does; it would make the steps the product's own parts).

Suggested timings, lengths and counts: adjust them to your own.

Who is the Demo Script Builder tool not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above, because the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want the tool to read their CRM, email or call notes: it works only from what you type.
  • Anyone who wants to send the output straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.

How do I use the Demo Script Builder tool with an AI assistant?

Add Revenue Enablement once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. Here is a prompt built from the tested example:

My role is not stated. Ask me for my role first. Use Revenue Enablement's Demo Script Builder tool. demo type: First look; your solution: our product; demo duration: 30; primary audience: Head of Last-Mile Operations; customer industry: Third-party logistics. If this tool is unavailable, ask me to open its web page; do not invent its output. How does the Demo Script Builder tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Demo Script Builder.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
