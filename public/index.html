Who is Revenue Enablement not ideal for?

  • Teams that want the tools to read their CRM, email or call notes: they work only from what you type.
  • Sending anything straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.
  • Teams that need saved deal history: nothing is stored, so keep each result in your own deal record.

How do I use Revenue Enablement?

In your browser

  1. Pick a tool above.
  2. Fill in the form, or press Fill in a tested example to see how it works.
  3. Press Run. The result appears on the page. Copy it, edit it, use it.

In your AI assistant

  1. Add this MCP server address to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another assistant: https://revenue-enablement.gtmhelix.com/mcp
  2. Choose no sign-in. The Connect page has the steps for each assistant.
  3. Ask in plain words, for example: Use Revenue Enablement to build an account plan for Northwind Clinics, a healthcare customer paying $24,000 a year for our ClinicFlow scheduling product.

What is Revenue Enablement built on?

Every tool returns a draft for a person to review before anything reaches a buyer. The tools do not see your CRM; they work only from what you type.

Questions and answers

What is Revenue Enablement?

Give it an account, a deal or a competitor, and it drafts account plans, deal strategy, discovery questions, ROI business cases, mutual action plans, win-loss reviews, proposal sections, emails, demo scripts, negotiation guides, champion kits and competitive traps.

Is Revenue Enablement free, and do I need an account?

It is free, and there is no account or sign-in. Use the tools in your browser here, or connect the MCP server to your AI assistant.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. Nothing is stored and nothing you type is logged. The privacy page has the details.

How do I use Revenue Enablement in Claude?

In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, select + and then Add custom connector. Name it Revenue Enablement, paste https://revenue-enablement.gtmhelix.com/mcp and choose no sign-in. The Connect page covers Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code and Windsurf too.
