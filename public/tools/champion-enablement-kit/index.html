Champion Enablement Kit

Create internal selling tools for your champion

For account executives with a champion inside the buyer. The decision: what does our champion need to sell this internally?

Give it the asset your champion needs and your solution, and get a draft of that asset, such as an executive brief with the opportunity, the business case, the investment and a recommendation.

How do I write an executive brief for a B2B champion to send to their CFO?

Write a one-page CFO brief in your champion's voice across five sections: the quantified operational problem and cost of inaction, what your solution changes, the annual budget required and approval threshold, the acceptance test criteria, and a low-risk pilot recommendation before full commitment. How to read the answer

The tool drafts an internal selling document written from your champion to their target stakeholder (such as an Executive Brief for CFO with Recommendation, What it is, What we expect to get, What it costs, Decision requested, About the reader, and Business case confirmation). Because your champion cannot recommend a purchase until cost, value, and decision authority are verified, the draft requests validation of the business case and a scoped pilot rather than assuming final approval, and it leaves out any ROI percentage unless you calculated it from the buyer's own cost figures.

What to do next

  • Founder: Review Business case confirmation with your champion to confirm the approved budget amount, the baseline metric, and who actually has authority to sign.
  • CEO: Offer to join a brief executive alignment call with the target stakeholder (such as the CFO) once the champion has shared the brief internally.
  • CMO: Make sure any vendor figure cited under What we expect to get is clearly labelled with its source and never presented as a result already measured at the buyer's company.
  • CRO: Have the account owner complete the About the reader check with the champion before the brief is forwarded: ask which acceptance condition the CFO will check and include that condition and its evidence in the brief.
  • Demand generation head: Pair this internal brief with a one-page case study from a peer company in the same sector so the champion has external proof alongside the internal pilot request.

Why come back

Re-run the kit with Known objections, Competitive context, Urgency drivers, and Champion name filled in, or switch the Asset type (Internal business case, Objection responses, Presentation talking points, Email to a stakeholder, ROI one-pager, Competitive comparison, or Risk assessment) as the deal advances. It runs the same fixed rules every time in your browser or in your own assistant, and your inputs are not stored or sent to an AI model.

Who is the Champion Enablement Kit tool not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above, because the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want the tool to read their CRM, email or call notes: it works only from what you type.
  • Anyone who wants to send the output straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.

How does the Champion Enablement Kit tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Champion Enablement Kit.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
