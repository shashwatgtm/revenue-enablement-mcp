Executive Brief for CFO

Topic: Lanehop

Solution: Lanehop

The Opportunity

Lanehop is the solution this document recommends.

Key Benefits:

  • Routes re-planned in under a minute when orders change
  • One dispatch view across all 12 cities
  • Dispatchers stop re-planning routes by hand
The Business Case

What happens today: No current way of working was given (Competitive context).

What it costs: put it in the buyer's own numbers; In logistics tech the case is usually judged on cost per delivery, first-attempt delivery rate, on-time delivery and deliveries per vehicle per day. The ROI Business Case Builder tool turns their cost figures into a return.

Solution: Lanehop. The outcome sought: routes re-planned in under a minute when orders change.

What finance leaders look for: how it posts to the ledger, what controls it adds and which manual steps the team stops doing.

Investment & Return

$90,000 a year from the operations budget; the CFO approves spend above $50,000

Recommendation

Proceed with Lanehop. The outcome sought: routes re-planned in under a minute when orders change.

Why now: no urgency driver was given (Urgency drivers). Name the date, renewal, audit or target that sets the timing.

Who is Champion Enablement Kit not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above; the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want the tools to read their CRM, email or call notes: they work only from what you type.
  • Sending anything straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.

How do I use Champion Enablement Kit from Claude?

Add Revenue Enablement once (see Use it in Claude or ChatGPT), then ask in plain words. A prompt built from the tested example:

Use Revenue Enablement's Champion Enablement Kit tool. asset type: Executive brief; your solution: Lanehop; champion role: Head of Last-Mile Operations; target stakeholder: CFO; key value points: routes re-planned in under a minute when orders change, one dispatch view across all 12 cities and dispatchers stop re-planning routes by hand; budget context: $90,000 a year from the operations budget; the CFO approves spend above $50,000.

How does Champion Enablement Kit work?

No sign-in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run Champion Enablement Kit.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
