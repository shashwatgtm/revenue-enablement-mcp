The stated reason. The reason the buyer gave, in the words you recorded: "price".

What the reason points to.

  • Price or budget: at $36,000 the account owner needs the buyer’s approved cost case against an enterprise route optimisation suite. Check whether the value case was agreed in the buyer's own numbers before price came up.
Questions for the review call
  1. What did the buyer say about an enterprise route optimisation suite, and at which point did it become the real comparison?
  2. The deal ran 75 days: which stage took longest, and was that the buyer's process or a stall you could have moved?
  3. The deal was worth $36,000: did the price or the size of the commitment come up as a reason, and who raised it?
  4. Did the buyer judge the result on cost per delivery, first attempt delivery rate and on time delivery, or on something else?
  5. Was the reason you recorded the real reason or the polite one, and who inside the buyer first said it?

Reading the answer

The analysis supports investigating the lost decision for route planning software for last-mile delivery. This sample cannot establish a general cause or justify changing the entire sales motion. Compare deals with the same segment, scope and period and retain contrary cases. The conclusion needs review if the buyer gives a different reason or comparable wins, losses and deferred decisions do not show the proposed pattern.

Decisions by role

Proposed owners. Confirm the named person before acting.

Founder: compare the reported reason "price" with a comparable win and loss for route planning software for last-mile delivery before changing the motion; retain cases that contradict it.

CEO: have finance compare the pursuit cost and 75 day cycle for the 36,000 value opportunity before funding a process change; do not generalize one deal's economics.

CMO: verify "price" with attributed buyer notes before changing a claim or campaign; separate buyer evidence from seller inference.

CRO: ask the recorded contacts for access to the actual decision maker and confirm the reason independently; record whether authority and the approval path were covered.

Demand generation head: test the proposed route planning software for last-mile delivery selection change on a separate, comparable account cohort; agree the measure and stop rule before comparing outcomes with the current criteria.

How to read the answer

The analyzer summarizes the deal facts you entered into a Write-up (What was sold, What the buyer weighed, The stated reason, and What the reason points to) and generates specific Questions for the review call based on your outcome, deal value, sales cycle length, stated reason, and winning competitor. It treats the stated loss reason (such as "price") as a hypothesis to check: for example, asking whether the value case was agreed in the buyer's own numbers before price came up, and whether the recorded reason was the real reason or the polite one.

What to do next

  • Founder: Call the buyer contact yourself using the Questions for the review call to find out at which point the winning alternative became the real comparison and whether your stated reason was the real reason.
  • CEO: Compare Sales Cycle length and Deal Value across your won and lost deals this quarter to see whether stalls happen in the buyer's process or because the economic buyer was never reached.
  • CMO: Feed the verified win and loss reasons into ICP Evolution Tracker and Competitive Intel so your positioning and battle cards reflect why buyers actually chose or rejected you.
  • CRO: Require reps to enter Stakeholders involved and Deal details (as listed under To sharpen this) before every post-deal debrief, and check whether an approved cost case existed before pricing was shared.
  • Demand generation head: Review lost-deal patterns (Several deals or Loss pattern mode) to stop spending budget on segments or triggers that consistently lose on the same structural gap.

Why come back

Run the analyzer after every closed-won or closed-lost deal in Single deal mode, and come back at the end of the quarter in Several deals, Competitor analysis, or Loss pattern mode with a portfolio summary of your deals. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.
