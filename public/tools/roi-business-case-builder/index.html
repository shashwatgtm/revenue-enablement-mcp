How this is worked out

CAC = spend / new customers. Monthly ARR per customer = annual revenue per customer / 12. Payback = 12 x spend / (new customers x annual revenue per customer). Gross margin payback = payback / (gross margin / 100). Recovery = sales cycle + payback.

Required annual revenue = 12 x spend / limit. Required customers are rounded up. Required opportunities = required customers / (win rate / 100), rounded up.

Months are rounded half up to one decimal before comparison with the limit. Money results are rounded to the nearest whole unit. Counts are rounded up.

Demand programmes pass at the limit; up to twice the limit a smaller test could pass. A long build passes up to twice the limit. Beyond twice the limit neither passes. Brand, software and unmatched GTM work have no funding verdict.

Rules of this tool: default 12 month limit; half and one and a half times the revenue; twice the limit for a smaller test or long build; under 0.5 months, above 120 months or required customers above 100 times those expected needs checking; limit from 1 to 60 months; cycle at most 36 months; gross margin and win rate above 0 and at most 100 percent.

Recovery is counted from the first day of the start month. Add sales cycle and payback, then round down to whole months to find the recovery month. No currency conversion.
