How do I build a B2B ROI business case that a buyer CFO will accept?

Build the case from the buyer's own baseline figures: what the problem costs them in a year today (current annual cost) and the share they expect to remove (expected improvement percent), or their direct annual value estimate. When you supply those figures alongside your solution price, ROI Business Case Builder calculates annual value, ROI percentage, payback period, 3-Year Net Value, and sensitivity scenarios at Conservative (50% of projected value) and Aggressive (150% of projected value); when buyer figures are missing, it shows no ROI and gives the price break-even thresholds and the questions to ask the buyer.

How do I show the return on an ABM programme or other marketing spend without inventing pipeline?

Do not invent industry averages or turn the buyer's annual revenue or employee count into pipeline on your own. Ask the buyer for their own figures: what the problem costs them in a year today (such as paid media that reaches the wrong accounts, or sales time spent on accounts that never buy) and the share they expect to remove, or the annual value they expect. Enter them with your annual price in ROI Business Case Builder to calculate net annual benefit, 3-Year Net Value, payback period, and the 50% Conservative and 150% Aggressive scenarios.

Reading the answer

The calculation supports checking the supplied account-based marketing economics. A calculated benefit is conditional on the supplied scope, price and improvement. Released capacity is not cash unless a cost is removed or additional contribution is evidenced. The conclusion needs review if the verified baseline, achievable improvement, recurring fee or implementation cost changes, or capacity cannot be converted into a supported benefit.

Decisions by role

Proposed owners. Confirm the named person before acting.

Founder: challenge whether the 30% improvement is achievable for account-based marketing before endorsing the case; revise scope or price if annual value does not cover the recurring fee.

CEO: have finance verify the 900,000 annual cost baseline, 120,000 price period and implementation cost before committing; approve only a benefit that finance can substantiate.

CMO: check "About 60% of paid media spend reaches companies outside the 40 target accounts; win rate on target accounts is 12% today" before publishing a return claim for account-based marketing; do not present labour capacity as an approved cash saving.

CRO: agree a buyer measurement of the 30% improvement against the 900,000 baseline before revising the proposal; require the same scope and period.

Demand generation head: keep this account-based marketing case out of positive return campaigns until the buyer confirms realized benefit and the 120,000 price period; retain any negative economics alongside it.

How to read the answer

When you enter your Solution price (for example $120,000 a year) without the buyer's Current annual cost and Expected improvement percent (or Annual value estimate), this tool refuses to invent an ROI percentage, payback period, or industry benchmark saving. Instead, it calculates the exact break-even and 2x return thresholds from your price ($120,000 a year for positive return, $240,000 for 2x return, $360,000 over three years), explains what a finance reviewer in the buyer's industry will check, and lists The numbers to ask for from the buyer. Once you enter the buyer's own cost and improvement percentage (or annual value estimate), it calculates the 1-year and 3-year net return, payback months, and sensitivity cases at 0.5x and 1.5x of the expected value.

What to do next

  • Founder: Take The numbers to ask for into your next meeting with the champion: ask what the problem costs the buyer in a year today (Current annual cost) and what share they expect to remove (Expected improvement percent), then re-run the builder.
  • CEO: Never send an ROI deck built on made-up industry averages to a CFO; insist that every business case rests on the buyer's own baseline figures and is checked by the buyer's finance contact first.
  • CMO: Add Known metrics from your existing customers so reps have labelled reference points from peer companies to share while asking the buyer for their own numbers.
  • CRO: Require reps to fill in Current process, Current annual cost, and Expected improvement percent with the champion and have the buyer's finance contact validate the sensitivity table before presenting to the economic buyer.
  • Demand generation head: Use the break-even arithmetic and What this buyer will check framework in mid-funnel ROI workshops and value assessment guides.

Why come back

Come back immediately after your champion or their finance contact gives you their Current annual cost and Expected improvement percent (or Annual value estimate) to generate the full ROI, payback period, and 0.5x/1.5x sensitivity analysis. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.

Which software builds an ROI business case?

The method matters more than the software. Write down what the buyer pays, the one value driver you can measure, today's baseline and the figure the buyer's finance team will check, then show the return at a low, an expected and a high case.

Helix does not rank vendors or name software to buy. Run this free builder for a first draft, then check that every assumption has a source, an owner on the buyer's side and a date before you send it.
