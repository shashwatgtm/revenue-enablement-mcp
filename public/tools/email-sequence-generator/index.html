What do I need to run Email Sequence Generator?

No sign-up. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

You need: Sequence type, Target persona, Your solution. You can add: Target industry, Key value prop, Specific pain point, Social proof, Call to action, Tone, Sender context.

  1. Fill in the form below, or press Fill in a tested example.
  2. Press Run Email Sequence Generator.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

Cold Outreach Sequence

Target: clinic operations directors in healthcare

Solution: ExampleCo Scheduler

Tone: Formal, polished, business-appropriate

Emails: 5 (default)

Email 1: The Opening (Day 1)

Subject Options:

  • Question about [their company's] [relevant initiative]
  • Quick thought on [pain point]
  • [Mutual connection] suggested I reach out

Body:

I've been following [Company]'s [relevant news/initiative] and noticed [observation].

Many clinic operations directors I speak with tell me [common pain point] is a top priority this year.

We help companies like yours [key outcome].

Best,

Sequence Tips

Timing:

  • Day 1 → 3 → 7 → 12 → 17
  • Adjust based on response patterns

Subject Lines:

  • Keep under 50 characters
  • No spam trigger words
  • Personalization when possible

Best Practices:

  • Research before sending
  • Personalize at least one element per email
  • Track open and reply rates
  • A/B test subject lines

Suggested timings, lengths and counts: adjust them to your own.

Who is Email Sequence Generator not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above; the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that want the tools to read their CRM, email or call notes: they work only from what you type.
  • Sending anything straight to a buyer: every output is a draft for a person to review first.

How do I use Email Sequence Generator from Claude?

Connect Revenue Enablement once (see Connect), then ask in plain words. A prompt built from the tested example:

Use Revenue Enablement's Email Sequence Generator tool. sequence type: Cold outreach; target persona: clinic operations directors; target industry: healthcare; your solution: ExampleCo Scheduler; tone: Professional.

How does Email Sequence Generator work?

It applies fixed rules in the Revenue Enablement code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
